# TakeMeter label definitions, copied word for word from the README's Label
# Taxonomy. baseline.py reads this file and slots each definition into the
# hypothesis "This post is {}.", which is why each one is a noun phrase.

analysis: an argument that offers a specific, checkable fact as its reason: a stat, a record, a date, a named game or play, a contract figure, or a quote
take: an opinion, judgment or prediction that someone could argue with, offered without a checkable fact as its reason
banter: a joke, a bit, a jab or a quick feeling that doesn't commit to any position someone could argue with
